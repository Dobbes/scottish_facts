Program policies · September 8, 2026

Terms and conditions

Scotland Facts is operated by Elumsden Sole. It is a personal, invitation-only informational and entertainment SMS program maintained through Dobbes/scottish_facts. It sends researched facts about Scotland with short humorous endings to one known consenting recipient.

Voluntary participation

By affirmatively agreeing in a direct conversation with the operator, you consent to automated SMS messages from Scotland Facts at the number you provide. Consent is not a condition of purchase. No purchased lists, unsolicited enrollment, website signup, or public issue enrollment are used. See the verbal enrollment disclosure.

Frequency, rates, and delivery

Message frequency is up to one fact SMS per day. A separate enrollment confirmation and requested service replies may also be sent. Message and data rates may apply under your mobile plan. A message can use multiple billable SMS segments even when it appears as one message on your device.

Delivery and timing are subject to provider and carrier availability and are not guaranteed. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. The program may be paused or discontinued.

STOP, HELP, and renewal

Reply STOP at any time to opt out through the sender's provider controls. A provider opt-out acknowledgement may be sent. You can also withdraw through your existing private conversation with the operator. An already submitted message cannot be recalled.

The application does not read inbound replies automatically. The operator must review incoming messages and support requests before daily sending, disable sending after withdrawal, and manually persist application suppression. Provider opt-out handling and application suppression are separate controls.

Reply HELP for the sender's provider-configured help response, or email brunslx@gmail.com for support or to withdraw consent. You may also use the existing private enrollment conversation. Do not post personal information, phone numbers, or account details in public repository issues.

START or UNSTOP may remove the provider's messaging block if supported for your sender. Even if the provider says you are resubscribed, this does not by itself restart Scotland Facts. Fresh affirmative consent and manual operator renewal are required before program messages resume.

Content and privacy

Facts use cited research and automated validation, but completeness and accuracy are not guaranteed. Humorous endings are entertainment, not actual subscription obligations. You can leave at any time.

The privacy policy explains mobile information, consent records, service providers, and retention after opt-out. Material changes to these terms will be reflected here with an updated date; materially changed enrollment disclosures require renewed consent before continuing under them.
