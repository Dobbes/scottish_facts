Privacy policy

Scotland Facts is operated by Elumsden Sole. It is an invitation-only informational SMS program maintained through Dobbes/scottish_facts. This policy describes the program and this static information site.

Information and purpose

The operator obtains the recipient's mobile number and affirmative consent directly, not through this website. The number is used for message delivery, support, opt-out handling, and delivery troubleshooting. A private consent and withdrawal record is kept separately from the public repository and the application database.

The recipient's number belongs in restricted local and hosted configuration, not in application database rows, source code, or OpenAI prompts. The database retains fact and SMS text, citations, generation records, delivery identifiers, timestamps, error codes, and a phone-free subscription suppression record. These support duplicate prevention, delivery reconciliation, and operations.

No marketing sharing

Scotland Facts does not sell, rent, or share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties, except service providers necessary to operate and support the messaging program. This exception does not permit marketing or promotional use.

Twilio and telecommunications carriers process phone numbers, message bodies, replies, and delivery metadata to provide messaging and opt-out controls. GitHub provides repository hosting, this site's hosting when published with Pages, and hosted job configuration. Supabase hosts operational database records. OpenAI processes fact research and style content, not recipient numbers or private consent records; research and style requests use storage disabled. Providers may retain service data under their own terms and policies.

Opt-out and retention

Reply STOP to opt out through the sender's provider controls. The application has no inbound webhook. Operator handling is manual: review incoming messages and support requests before daily sending, disable sending, persist suppression, and remove the recipient number from local and hosted configuration after withdrawal. Observed Twilio opt-out errors also trigger application suppression as a fallback.

Removal is not automatic or instantaneous, and it does not erase messages already submitted or delivered. Configuration can remain until the operator removes it. Private consent and withdrawal evidence, phone-free suppression, and operational audit history may remain after opt-out for consent compliance, duplicate prevention, and troubleshooting. There is no automatic retention expiry or deletion job. The operator reviews private records and removes information no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations.

Twilio message logs, carrier records, GitHub service data, and provider backups may remain under provider retention practices; this application cannot promise their immediate deletion. See Twilio's privacy notice and GitHub's privacy statement.

This website and support

This site has no signup form, application cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party embedded content. The hosting provider may process standard request data such as IP addresses for serving and securing the site. Following external links is subject to the destination's policies.

For privacy questions or a request to review or remove information, email Elumsden Sole at brunslx@gmail.com or use your existing private enrollment conversation. Support email and its email provider process your address and the information you choose to include to handle your request; provide only what is necessary and never send credentials. Do not post personal information, phone numbers, consent evidence, or account details in public repository issues.

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date. See the messaging terms and enrollment disclosure.
