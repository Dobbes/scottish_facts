By invitation. By agreement.

Scotland Facts uses verbal enrollment in a direct conversation with its one known recipient. Visiting this page does not subscribe you.

Scotland Facts is operated by Elumsden Sole. Before requesting consent, the operator identifies themselves as Elumsden Sole, identifies the sending number privately, and provides access to the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. The recipient can read them and ask questions before deciding.

Verbal consent script

I am Elumsden Sole, the operator of Scotland Facts, an invitation-only program that sends researched facts about Scotland with a short humorous ending. Would you like to receive automated Scotland Facts SMS messages at the mobile number you provide?

You will receive up to one fact SMS per day. A separate enrollment confirmation and requested service replies may also be sent. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase, and you can decline.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or email brunslx@gmail.com for support or to withdraw consent. You can also contact me through this private conversation.

START or UNSTOP may remove a provider block but will not by itself restart Scotland Facts. You must give fresh consent and I must manually renew the subscription.

The Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy are linked on this page. They explain the program, mobile information use, service providers, and retention. Have you had an opportunity to review them, and do you affirmatively agree to receive these automated messages?

What counts as consent

The operator waits for an unambiguous affirmative answer. Silence, a prior relationship, possession of a phone number, or viewing this website is not consent. There is no enrollment keyword and no form on this site. Do not submit a number or consent through a public GitHub issue.

Private record procedure

Before enabling delivery, the operator records the date, time and timezone, participant and operator identity, privately verified recipient and sender numbers, the disclosure version and policy links shared, and the recipient's affirmative response in a restricted record outside this repository and application database. Do not publish the log or a screenshot of it. An audio recording is not required by this procedure.

Withdrawals and any later renewed consent are recorded privately as well. After withdrawal, the operator disables sending, persists the phone-free application suppression, and removes recipient configuration. Renewal requires fresh consent and the provider's applicable opt-in process, followed by manual application renewal.

Confirmation and service replies

The daily application does not automatically send enrollment confirmations or process inbound messages. An enrollment confirmation, when required, is handled separately by the operator through an approved provider procedure after consent and before daily messaging. Provider STOP, START, and HELP responses depend on the sender's configuration. These service replies are separate from daily fact messages.

Questions? Email brunslx@gmail.com or use the existing private enrollment conversation. Do not post personal information in public repository issues.
