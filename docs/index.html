A little more Scotland. One fact at a time.

Source-backed facts about Scotland, followed by a small dose of absurd humor. For one known recipient who has directly agreed to receive them.

The essentials

Receive up to one automated fact SMS per day. A separate enrollment confirmation and requested service replies may also be sent. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help through the sender's provider-configured response, or email brunslx@gmail.com. You can also contact the operator through your existing private enrollment conversation.
